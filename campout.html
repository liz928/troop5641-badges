Safety plan

  • Headcount at every transition: after the hike, before + after the fire, bedtime, morning.
  • Buddies always, including night bathroom trips (wake your buddy, then an adult).
  • Whistle: 3 blasts = everyone to the meeting spot.
  • Fire: adult lights + tends it the whole time. Water bucket + shovel ready first. Hair tied back, no ponchos, roast sitting down. No flames or heaters in tents.
  • Meds held by Liz, given per the health form, logged.
  • Lightning or thunder: everyone into the house. Wait 30 min after the last thunder.
  • Cold kid: dry clothes, spare bag or blankets, warm drink, call home if needed.
  • Injury: check the scene, 911 if needed (site address above), first aid, call the parent.
